Understanding adult acne: common causes and treatment options

Published by DermaGlow Dermatology Clinic

Adult acne can affect people at different stages of life. It may be influenced by hormones, genetics, skin care products and other factors.

Why does acne happen?

Acne develops when hair follicles become blocked with oil and dead skin cells. Inflammation can then lead to different types of lesions.

When should you see a dermatologist?

If acne is persistent, painful, leaving marks or affecting your confidence, a dermatologist can assess your skin and discuss appropriate treatment options.

Treatment is personal

There is no single treatment approach that works for everyone. Your dermatologist may consider your skin type, severity, history and goals when developing a plan.
